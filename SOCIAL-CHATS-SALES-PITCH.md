## Objection Handling

### "It's too expensive"

```
Totally get that! Quick question though — what's one new customer worth to you?

If a single booking pays for 3+ months of Social-Chats AI, it basically pays for 
itself. And most clients see 5-10 bookings in the first month alone.

Think of it less as a cost and more as a salesperson who works 24/7 for less than 
€3/day.

Does that make sense?
```

### "I'll just do it myself"

```
You absolutely could! And if you have the time, that's a great option.

Quick reality check though — how many DMs do you currently reply to within 5 minutes? 
(Studies show 78% of buyers choose the first responder.)

Social-Chats AI isn't about replacing you — it's about handling the instant replies 
so you can focus on running the business. You still get handed over every warm lead 
for the final close.

What's your time worth per hour? If it's more than €3/hour, this basically pays 
for itself.
```

### "Will it sound like me?"

```
Great question! Yes — that's actually the secret sauce.

We train the AI on:
- Your actual conversations (past DMs, emails, chats)
- Your website + social media content
- Your brand voice guidelines (professional, friendly, fun, etc.)

You approve every message template before it goes live. And if it ever says 
something off-brand, you can flag it and we retrain instantly.

Want to see examples from similar businesses? I can send you some real conversations.
```

### "What if it says something wrong?"

```
Totally valid concern! Here's how we prevent that:

1. **Training:** We train it on YOUR actual conversations, not generic responses
2. **Approval:** You approve all templates before anything goes live
3. **Confidence flags:** If the AI is unsure (confidence <80%), it flags for your review
4. **Human handover:** Complex questions get passed to you instantly
5. **Continuous learning:** We retrain based on your feedback

You're always in control. The AI handles the easy stuff (80% of DMs), you handle 
the high-value conversations (20%).

Sound fair?
```

### "I need to think about it"

```
No problem at all! This is a big decision.

Two quick questions:
1. When should I follow up? (Next week? Next month?)
2. What specifically do you need to think through? (Pricing? Features? Timing?)

Also, here's a case study from [similar business] — they had the same concerns, 
and now they're booking 15-20 appointments/month through Social-Chats AI.

[Send case study link]

No pressure — just want to make sure you have all the info!
```

### "We already have someone handling DMs"

```
That's awesome! Quick question — how fast do they typically respond to DMs?

(Asking because the average business takes 6+ hours. Social-Chats AI responds in 
under 30 seconds, 24/7.)

Also, do they qualify leads before booking? Follow up with cold prospects? Work 
nights and weekends?

Most of our clients kept their existing team — Social-Chats AI handles the 
instant replies + follow-ups, and the team focuses on closing warm leads.

Want to see how it would work alongside your current setup?
```

3. **Goal:** Book demo calls / trial signups
4. **Sending Window:** 9 AM - 6 PM (local time)

### Custom Fields to Track

| Field | Type | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| `lead_source` | Text | Where did this lead come from? |
| `follower_count` | Number | Their social media following |
| `business_type` | Text | Industry (salon, coach, etc.) |
| `pain_point` | Text | What problem did they mention? |
| `objection` | Text | Why haven't they bought yet? |
| `last_contact` | Date | When did we last reach out? |
| `campaign_stage` | Dropdown | Prospect → Demo → Trial → Paid |

When lead replies "YES":
- Trigger: `reply_received`
- Action: Send demo video link
- Tag: `interested_lead`
- Notify: David (WhatsApp + email)
